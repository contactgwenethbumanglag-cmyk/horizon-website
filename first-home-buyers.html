First home buyer questions

How much deposit do I need? +−

Many lenders prefer 20%, but eligible first home buyers may buy with as little as 5% under the government scheme. We'll help you work out what suits you.

What is LMI? +−

Lenders Mortgage Insurance protects the lender if a loan isn't repaid. It's usually charged when your deposit is under 20%, though eligible buyers under the scheme may avoid it.

What is pre-approval? +−

It's a lender's conditional OK on how much you could borrow. It isn't a guarantee, but it helps you shop with confidence.

Can I use a grant? +−

It depends on your state, the property and whether it's new. We'll check which grants and concessions apply to you.
